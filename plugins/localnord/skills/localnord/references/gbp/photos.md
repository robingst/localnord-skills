# Photos and videos
Checked against Google's help pages on 2026-10-12.

## Which photos to add

Google's tips, with the minimum it suggests for each type:
- **Exterior**: at least 3, from each direction customers arrive, ideally at different times of day. It helps customers recognise the place.
- **Interior**: at least 3, showing what it feels like to stand inside as a customer.
- **Products**: at least 3 of the most popular items, evenly lit.
- **At work**: at least 3 showing the services, with the team serving customers.
- **Team**: at least 3 of management and employees. A less formal style shows personality.
- **Food and drink**: at least 3. **Common areas**: at least 1 per area. **Rooms**: at least 3 of the most popular room types.
- **Logo and cover photo**: the cover sits at the top of the profile but is not guaranteed to show first. A low-quality cover, or one judged a poor representation, may be replaced by a customer photo.

## Photo format, size and quality

- Format: JPG or PNG. Convert HEIC or WebP files first.
- File size: between 10 KB and 5 MB.
- Resolution: 720 x 720 px recommended, 250 x 250 px minimum.
- In focus and well lit. No significant alterations, no excessive filters or AI: the image must show the real place.

## Videos

- Up to 30 seconds.
- Up to 75 MB.
- 720p resolution or higher.
- Stable footage: avoid shaky, out-of-focus or distorted videos.

## What Google refuses or removes

- Anything that breaks Google's prohibited and restricted content policy.
- Google's media tips ask to avoid: stock photos, screenshots, GIFs, collages, images made by others, added borders or text, heavily edited or manipulated images, blurry, very dark or rotated images, selfies centred on a person rather than the place.
- Low-quality media may be removed, and quality removals cannot be appealed.
- Photos show only once the profile is verified. New media can take 24 to 48 hours to appear. Statuses: Pending, Not approved, Live.
- An upload that is a duplicate of a photo already live may not be published.
- Restricted media can be appealed in the appeals tool in the UK and the EEA, and through Google's contact form elsewhere.

## Regularity

Practice, not a Google rule:
- Add a few real photos regularly (new work, season, new products, team changes) rather than one large batch.
- Replace photos that no longer match the place: renovation, new storefront, new signage.
- Every photo shows this location and this business.
- A service business that works at customers' premises leans on "at work" photos of real jobs.
- Get consent before publishing a recognisable customer or employee.

## With the connector

- `analyze_photo_coverage`: counts published photos per category and lists the missing core ones (cover, profile, exterior, interior, plus those expected for the business type).
- `upload_photo_google`: publishes a photo already saved as a draft in Localnord, with its category. Show the photo and its category first, and publish only after an explicit "yes".
- A missing category is a request for the client, not a reason to use stock or AI images.

## Without the connector

Ask how many photos the profile has per type and when the last one was added. Compare with the list above and give the client a short shot list: what to photograph, where, at what time of day.
