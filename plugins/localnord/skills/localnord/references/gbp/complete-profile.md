# Complete profile checklist
Checked against Google's help pages on 2026-10-12.

Use this list for a new client listing or an audit. Check each line against the real business, not against competitors. Most urgent first: anything that can get the profile suspended (name, address), then what customers use (hours, phone), then the rest.

## Identity

- [ ] **Name**: the real-world name, as on signage, website and stationery. No keywords, city or neighbourhood, services, slogans, store codes, trademark signs, all-caps words, hours, phone numbers or URLs. Legal forms (Ltd, Inc) only if the business uses them consistently. Extra words can lead to suspension. Changing the name after verification may require verifying again.
- [ ] **Categories**: one primary category, the most specific that describes the business as a whole, plus as few additional ones as possible (up to 9). See the categories guide.
- [ ] **Verification**: the profile is verified. Unverified profiles may have content rejected, and their photos do not show.

## Address or service area

- [ ] **Address**: precise and real, with permanent signage bearing the business name. Suite or unit in address line 2. No P.O. box, remote mailbox or virtual office. A co-working space only with signage, staff and customers received during business hours. One profile per location.
- [ ] **Service area**: a business that visits or delivers to customers and does not receive them removes its address and sets a service area. Up to 20 service areas, not more than about 2 hours of driving from the base.
- [ ] **Hybrid business**: receives customers at its address and also travels: show both the address and the service area.
- [ ] Moving after verification means verifying the business again.

## Hours

- [ ] **Regular hours**: the hours customers are served. Seasonal businesses mark themselves temporarily closed off-season.
- [ ] Some businesses should not list hours: varied hours (classes, showtimes, worship services), appointment-only businesses, hotels, schools, transport services, event venues.
- [ ] **Several sets of hours**: use the customer-facing ones (lobby hours for a bank, dine-in hours for a restaurant). Specific services go in "More hours" (delivery, takeout, senior hours).
- [ ] **Special hours**: holidays, events, or a closure of up to 6 days in a row. Confirm hours for official holidays even when they are unchanged.
- [ ] **Temporarily closed**: for 7 days in a row or more, or an unknown period.

## Contact

- [ ] **Phone**: a local number for this location, under the business's direct control, not a premium-rate number, not a redirect. A central call centre only if there is no other choice. Up to 2 additional numbers.
- [ ] **Website**: the page that represents this location, full URL with http:// or https://. No redirect to another landing page or to a social media page.
- [ ] **Social media links**: one per platform.

## Content

- [ ] **Description**: at most 750 characters. What the business offers, what makes it different, its history. No URLs, HTML or links, no promotions, prices or sales. Honest and relevant.
- [ ] **Services**: available for some categories. Prefer the services Google suggests; for a custom service, no profanity, gibberish, personal information, prices or phone numbers in its name.
- [ ] **Attributes**: answer Yes only when it is true at the place. Accessibility attributes have precise criteria, for example a wheelchair-accessible entrance is about 1 meter wide with no steps, and revolving doors only means No. Some attributes come from customers' answers.
- [ ] **Opening date**: month and year.
- [ ] **Photos**: logo, cover, exterior, interior, at work, team, products. See the photos guide. The logo is highlighted when name, address, categories, phone and hours are filled in.

## With the connector

- `get_listing_overview`: the listing as Localnord sees it: hours, special hours, categories, services, attributes, photos per category, description, phone, website. Check it line by line against this list.
- `run_full_audit`: a one-call audit (score, unreplied reviews, posts, photo coverage, statistics) with the top priorities.
- Any correction on Google is a listing edit: show the before/after for each field and wait for an explicit "yes".
- Warn before changing the name, categories or address: Google reviews every edit before it goes live, and these changes may require verifying the business again.

## Without the connector

Ask for screenshots of the profile, or for each field above. Return the checklist with one status per line (OK, to fix, missing), most urgent first, and the exact text to enter where it is useful.
